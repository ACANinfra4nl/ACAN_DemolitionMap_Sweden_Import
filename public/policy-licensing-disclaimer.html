Policy, Licensing & Disclaimer

This page summarizes the current governance and licensing approach of the ACAN Demolition Atlas.

Database license

The atlas database is intended to be published under ODbL 1.0. This applies to structured data such as addresses, coordinates, demolition status, and related metadata.

License text: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

Photos and contributed media

Submitted photos are intended to be licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Linked or externally sourced images keep their original license and copyright status.

License text: Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0.

Accuracy disclaimer

Although volunteers and non-profit contributors follow a protocol to check building entries to the best of their capacity, ACAN Netherlands cannot guarantee the absolute correctness or completeness of all published information.

Non-commercial intent

The Sloopkaart / Demolition Atlas is made available for research, heritage advocacy, and public-interest purposes. Use for commercial or for-profit purposes, including commercial demolition or speculative exploitation, is not aligned with the project intent.

Privacy and governance note

Personal data submitted through contribution workflows is governed under GDPR obligations and handled separately from publicly published map data.

For more information requests, please reach out by email via info@sloopkaart.nl.
